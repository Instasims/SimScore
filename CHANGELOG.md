# SimScore

Each release carries the day's scores. Changes to the addon itself:

## 1.4.0

- Every rogue, mage and warlock tree, Elemental and Enhancement shamans, and Balance and Feral druids, each with its
  class icon and its tree's.

## 1.3.0

- Marksmanship and Survival hunters, with the hunter icon and each tree's.

## 1.2.0

- Retribution paladins, with the paladin and Retribution icons.

## 1.1.0

- Shadow priests: a level-60 Shadow priest's SimScore, with the priest and Shadow icons.

## 1.0.0

- A player's SimScore in their tooltip: how their gear and talents sim against every level-60 Fury warrior on
  Forever, coloured like Warcraft Logs' percentiles.
- Each spec keeps the character's best score ever; a character scored in more than one spec shows each, best first.
